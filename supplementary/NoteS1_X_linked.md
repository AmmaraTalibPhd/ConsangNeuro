# Note S1. Exploratory estimate for X-linked genes

This note accompanies "Genomic reference under-representation obscures recessive neurological disease burden in consanguineous populations". X-linked genes fall outside the autosomal inbreeding model used in the main analysis and were excluded from it. The calculation below is exploratory and is not used in any result or conclusion.

For the 72 excluded X-linked genes we applied the same variant filters (≥1 review star, allele frequency ≤1% in every ancestry) and summed pathogenic allele frequencies per ancestry. In hemizygous males one pathogenic allele is sufficient, so the expected fraction of affected males is approximately this sum, and of all births half of it; affected females were ignored and full penetrance was assumed. The burden does not depend on parental relatedness because a male's single X chromosome comes from his mother. The wide range (about 90 per 100,000 for South Asian and 370 for Middle Eastern frequencies) mostly reflects sampling noise in small ancestry groups and a few genes with relatively common variants, such as *FGD1*. It is an order-of-magnitude indication only, because many pathogenic variants in these genes arise de novo and are under-represented in population samples.
